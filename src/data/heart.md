# Cardiovascular Health & Cardiology Information

This document serves as the local medical reference for heart health, cardiovascular symptoms, standard surgical interventions, and preventive lifestyle recommendations.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Heart Health
The heart is a muscular organ responsible for pumping blood throughout the circulatory system. Maintaining its health involves:
* **Optimal Blood Pressure**: Ideally below 120/80 mmHg.
* **Balanced Cholesterol**: Keeping LDL ("bad" cholesterol) low and HDL ("good" cholesterol) high.
* **Active Circulation**: Engaging in cardiovascular exercise (at least 150 minutes per week).

---

## 2. Common Symptoms & Understanding Chest Pain
Understanding the nature of cardiovascular symptoms is key to early detection:

### Stable Angina
* **Cause**: Temporary reduction of blood flow to the heart muscle, usually due to plaque buildup in coronary arteries.
* **Sensation**: Squeezing, pressure, or fullness in the center of the chest. It often occurs during physical exertion or emotional stress.
* **Duration**: Typically lasts 5 minutes or less and resolves with rest or medication (like nitroglycerin).

### Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack)
* **Cause**: Sudden, complete blockage of a coronary artery, cutting off oxygenated blood to a section of the heart muscle.
* **Sensation**: Severe, crushing chest pain that may radiate to the left arm, neck, jaw, or back. Often accompanied by shortness of breath, cold sweating, nausea, and lightheadedness.
* **Duration**: Lasts longer than a few minutes and does not resolve with rest.
* **Action Required**: **IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY MEDICAL CARE.**

## 3. Standard Cardiac Surgical Interventions

### Coronary Angioplasty (PCI - Percutaneous Coronary Intervention)
* **Description**: A minimally invasive procedure used to open clogged heart arteries.
* **Procedure**: A thin catheter with a balloon at its tip is inserted through a blood vessel in the groin or wrist and guided to the blocked artery. The balloon is inflated to compress the plaque against the artery wall.
* **Stenting**: A tiny wire mesh tube (stent) is usually deployed to keep the artery permanently propped open. Most modern stents are drug-eluting, releasing medication to prevent scar tissue formation.

### Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)
* **Description**: A major surgical procedure to restore blood flow to heart muscles around severely blocked coronary arteries.
* **Procedure**: A healthy blood vessel (graft) is harvested from another part of the body (typically the leg, arm, or chest) and connected above and below the blocked coronary artery. This "bypasses" the blockage.
* **Indications**: Recommended for patients with multiple blockages or blockages in the left main coronary artery.

## 4. Lifestyle & Dietary Guidelines
Preventive cardiology focuses heavily on daily habits:
* **Cardio-Protective Diet**: Emphasize whole foods, leafy greens, berries, oats, olive oil, nuts, fish high in omega-3 fatty acids, and lean proteins. Limit sodium (salt) intake to reduce blood pressure.
* **Physical Fitness**: Incorporate aerobic exercises like walking, swimming, or cycling.
* **Stress Regulation**: Chronic stress elevates cortisol and blood pressure. Implement mindfulness, yoga, or deep-breathing exercises.
* **Smoking Cessation**: Smoking damages the endothelium (lining of blood vessels) and severely accelerates plaque buildup (atherosclerosis).

---

## 5. Critical Emergency Red Flags
If you experience any of the following symptoms, stop all activity and call emergency services immediately:
1. **Severe chest pain or pressure** (crushing or heavy feeling).
2. **Shortness of breath** or difficulty breathing.
3. **Pain radiating to the left arm**, shoulder, neck, or jaw.
4. **Sudden cold sweating**, dizziness, or extreme fatigue.
